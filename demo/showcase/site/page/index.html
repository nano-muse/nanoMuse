A phone in your browser, with a nanoMuse of your own on it. 浏览器里的一台手机，装着一个属于你的 nanoMuse。

A demo in a simulator. The phone and its apps are MobileGym's re-creations, and this nanoMuse is the lite web app. The apps for your own devices do far more; install one of them. 这是模拟器里的演示。手机和应用是 MobileGym 重做的，这个 nanoMuse 是精简版网页应用。装在你自己设备上的 App 能做的多得多，请安装一个。
