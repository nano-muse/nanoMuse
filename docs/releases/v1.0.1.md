# nanoMuse 1.0.1 · Ballast

🐉 **nanoMuse `1.0.1` “Ballast”**: the first day of 1.0 in people's hands, read back; what broke for them is fixed, and what two apps on one phone fought over is settled.

1.0.0 went out on 2026-10-09. Within a day the reports came in: a phone with OpenMinis installed asked which app should open every link, because both apps answered to the same `minis://`; a desktop chat filled by the phone's conversation could not be scrolled to its start ([#261](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/261)); the relay's Health page said the model provider needed a look when all that had happened was one long conversation that the provider's content check kept declining. This version fixes those three and a handful of things we found ourselves while using it: the glow around the screen that went dark between two steps of the hands, the small dragon that stood still on Windows, a switch that followed one rule on the phone and another on the desktop.

Nothing moves for a person upgrading: the Android package installs over 1.0.0 and keeps everything, the desktop keeps its settings, and a 1.0.0 client works against the new relay unchanged.

## Highlights

- **The phone app has its own URL scheme.** The system now knows nanoMuse as `nanomuse://` (and the iPhone's MCP sign-in callback as `nanomuse-mcp://`) instead of upstream's `minis://`, so a phone that also has OpenMinis installed no longer asks which app should open a link, a notification, a share or a shortcut of either; reported by OpenMinis' author, whose users hit the chooser. Inside the app nothing changes: the links the model writes, the Linux sandbox and the pages that read them keep their `minis://` form, and every link the app opens in itself is now addressed to the app by name rather than to whoever claims the scheme.
- **A content check saying no is one sentence, everywhere.** When the model provider's content check declines the words of a turn (nanoMuse Cloud's upstream, or the vendor behind a key of your own), Android, the iPhone, the desktop and the web console say so in your language and offer *New chat* and *Try again*; nothing retries it by itself. Before, the refusal looked like any other provider error, the compaction before a long conversation's next turn split its request in halves and tried again, and one such conversation was declined two hundred times in an hour. The relay (0.24.0) records these as `content.rejected` and the Health page counts them apart from upstream errors, since they say nothing about the provider. [docs/troubleshooting.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/troubleshooting.md) has an entry.
- **The desktop, in three places.** A chat this computer had not written in yet, filled by another device's conversation, scrolls to its start ([#261](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/261)). While the hands work on this computer, the breathing light round the screen and the pill at the top stay up from the turn's first screen call to its end; they used to follow each tool call and went dark while the model thought. The Windows installer now carries the four clips the avatar plays, so the small dragon moves on Windows as it has on the Mac and Linux since 0.1.32.
- **One turn on nanoMuse Cloud, on request.** When your own model fails a turn (a refused key, a timeout, no connection), the web console offers *Use nanoMuse Cloud this time* under the sentence, as the desktop and the phones already did: the same words again, one turn on the account's model, the next back on your key; the reply says which model answered. The runtime takes it as `via: "cloud"` on `POST /api/threads/{id}/send` and the socket's `send` frame.
- ***Use nanoMuse Cloud models* follows one rule on every client.** Off while the chat model is the account's, the Chat row moves to your first own chat model (the catalogue's default for that provider), on Android, the iPhone, the desktop and the web alike; with no chat model of your own the switch stays on and says *Add a provider of your own first; with nanoMuse Cloud off, nothing else could answer.* On the desktop the other half holds too: removing the last key of your own while the switch is off turns the account's models back on instead of dropping new chats onto dsh's stock default, and the row says so.
- **The runtime.** A task another device asked for and then stopped ends as `cancelled`, as the hub protocol says, instead of being answered with the conversation's previous reply; only the device that asked can stop it. The first feed day is written in the language of your screens rather than in English. Switching the model while a reply is on its way no longer breaks the turn; the new model takes over from the next one. A failed model test under Connections says what went wrong in plain words, with the provider's line under *Details*. The web console's approval card says why it asks in your language and lists the permissions you granted by risk, the gravest first.
- **The phones, besides.** Android: the sign-in page no longer calls a mainland-China number "outside the mainland" while it is still being typed; the hands notice when they go round in circles (a third repeat of one action on an unchanged screen is named in the history, a sixth ends the run); the welcome page says how a computer joins today. iPhone: the Version row finds the latest release from mainland China too; the first run's sign-in sheet closes by itself once the sign-in succeeds; a task another device hands the iPhone is answered in that device's language; the Chinese copy uses the same words as the Android app (智能体, 中继, 聊天); an error nanoMuse Cloud puts inside a stream ends the turn with its card instead of *Empty response*. Both: the Settings page's nanoMuse Cloud row says what is left of the allowance in yuan, the figure the account page leads with.
- **The relay, 0.24.0.** A clip whose task the app never polled to an end (the app was closed, the phone slept) is charged all the same, once, at the price fixed at submission; a relay without an upstream key no longer holds a place in the in-flight count for a request it refused, which locked an account out after four of them; the production self-check reports a missing or stale backup.
- **The TestFlight beta passed Apple's review.** Build 16 (1.0.0) is what the public link installs today; 1.0.1 goes up as build 18 and reaches the link once its review has passed. The install tables of the README, its nine translations and the docs say so.

## Upgrade Notes

- **Android installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your data.** `nanoMuse-1.0.1-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0+, arm64; versionCode 44) is signed with the same key as every version before it. Verify with `sha256sum -c nanoMuse-1.0.1-arm64.apk.sha256`.
- **The app's URL scheme changed.** A shortcut, an automation or a bookmark outside the app that opened `minis://…` should open `nanomuse://…` now (the path stays the same); the system no longer hands `minis://` to nanoMuse. Links inside the app, and the links the model writes, are untouched.
- **iPhone and iPad come through TestFlight.** The public link installs build 16 (1.0.0), which passed Apple's beta review; build 18 (1.0.1) is submitted for review when it is uploaded and reaches the link once it passes, the internal testers before that. On the iPhone the share extension and the MCP sign-in callback use the new schemes; nothing to do on your side.
- **The desktop installers are built by CI from the tag `v1.0.1`.** The Mac packages are not notarized: the first time, open the app from *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway* (or right-click → *Open*). The Windows package is not signed: click *More info* and *Run anyway* once. On Linux prefer the `.deb`; the AppImage needs FUSE (`--appimage-extract-and-run` without). They arrive a little after the APK, and `SHA256SUMS-desktop.txt` lists every checksum. The Windows package is larger than 1.0.0's by the four avatar clips it now carries.
- **The relay is 0.24.0; nothing changes on the wire.** A 1.0.0 client works against it unchanged. New for an operator: the events table carries `content.rejected` next to `upstream.error`, the Health page and the self-check report them apart, and the self-check (`cloud/deploy/nanomuse-hk/selfcheck.sh`) also alerts when the backup directory has no `cloud-*.db.gz` or the newest is older than `BACKUP_MAX_H` hours (48 by default); set `BACKUP_DIR` if yours is elsewhere. A clip left unpolled is settled by the relay itself, once, shortly before the hour's hold on the allowance lapses.

## Community

nanoMuse is open source, free and non-profit. Sign in with a phone number or an e-mail and you get a starting allowance; the developer pays for it. The account page shows what is left and how to add more. When it is gone, use your own key or a plan you already pay for ([how](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md)); the key you add can take over any of the four jobs, and the account can be switched off as a model source while you keep it for sync and your devices. With an account, the text of your main conversation is kept on nanoMuse Cloud so that your devices show the same chat, side chats only if you say so, and one switch in Data controls turns it off and deletes it; nothing is sold ([privacy policy](https://nanomuse.cn/privacy/)); delete the account whenever you want. The same relay runs on a server of yours: [docs/self-hosting.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md).

A stable version is not the last word. We use it every day and still know where it is rough; tell us where it broke for you and what you want it to do. The [roadmap](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md) says where help is wanted, and [docs/parity.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/parity.md) says what each client has. 🐉 **nanoMuse `1.0.1`「Ballast」**：1.0 到了大家手里的第一天，我们把反馈读了一遍；坏掉的修好了，两个 App 在同一部手机上抢的东西也分清了。

1.0.0 在 2026-10-09 发出。一天之内反馈就来了：装了 OpenMinis 的手机每打开一个链接都会被问「用哪个 App 打开」，因为两个 App 都认 `minis://`；桌面端里一条由手机对话填满的聊天滚不到开头（[#261](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/261)）；中继的健康页说模型服务商需要看看，而实际发生的只是一条长对话被服务商的内容审核反复拒绝。这个版本修了这三件事，还有一些我们自己用的时候发现的：操作屏幕时在两步之间熄掉的呼吸灯，Windows 上一动不动的小龙，在手机上是一条规则、到桌面上又是另一条规则的那个开关。

升级的人什么都不用挪：Android 安装包覆盖安装 1.0.0、数据全部保留，桌面端保留设置，1.0.0 的客户端对着新中继照常工作。

### 亮点

- **手机 App 有了自己的 URL scheme。** 系统现在用 `nanomuse://` 认 nanoMuse（iPhone 上 MCP 登录回调是 `nanomuse-mcp://`），不再用上游的 `minis://`，所以同时装了 OpenMinis 的手机打开链接、通知、分享或快捷方式时不再弹出选择框；这是 OpenMinis 的作者报告的，他的用户遇到了这个选择框。App 内部什么都没变：模型写出来的链接、Linux 沙箱和读它们的页面都保留 `minis://` 的形式，App 自己打开的每一个链接现在都按名字指向 App 本身，而不是交给任何声称认这个 scheme 的程序。
- **内容审核拒绝，到处都是一句话。** 模型服务商的内容审核不放行某一轮的文字时（nanoMuse Cloud 的上游，或者你自己 key 背后的厂商），Android、iPhone、桌面端和网页控制台都用你的语言说清楚，给出「新聊天」和「再试一次」；没有任何东西会自动重试。以前这种拒绝看起来和其他服务商错误一样，长对话下一轮之前的压缩会把请求对半拆开再试，一条这样的对话一小时内被拒绝了两百次。中继（0.24.0）把它们记作 `content.rejected`，健康页把它们和上游错误分开计数，因为它们说明不了服务商的状况。[docs/troubleshooting.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/zh/troubleshooting.md) 里有一条。
- **桌面端的三处。** 这台电脑还没写过、由别的设备的对话填满的聊天，能滚到开头了（[#261](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/261)）。操作屏幕的时候，屏幕四周的呼吸灯和顶上的胶囊从这一轮第一次读屏一直亮到结束；以前它们跟着每次工具调用走，模型思考的时候就熄了。Windows 安装包现在带上了形象播放的四段短片，所以小龙在 Windows 上也会动了，Mac 和 Linux 上从 0.1.32 起就是这样。
- **某一轮改走 nanoMuse Cloud。** 你自己的模型某一轮失败时（key 被拒、超时、没有网络），网页控制台在那句话下面给出「这次改用 nanoMuse Cloud」，桌面端和手机早就有：同样的话再发一次，这一轮走账号的模型，下一轮回到你的 key；回复会写明是哪个模型答的。运行时在 `POST /api/threads/{id}/send` 和 socket 的 `send` 帧上以 `via: "cloud"` 接收它。
- **「使用 nanoMuse Cloud 的模型」在每个客户端是同一条规则。** 对话模型还是账号的时候把它关掉，「对话」一行会移到你自己的第一个对话模型（目录里这家服务商的默认），Android、iPhone、桌面端和网页都一样；自己一个对话模型都没有时，开关保持打开并说「先添加一个你自己的服务商；关掉 nanoMuse Cloud 后就没有别的来源能回答了」。桌面端另一半也成立：开关关着的时候删掉最后一把自己的 key，账号的模型会重新打开，而不是把新聊天丢给 dsh 自带的默认模型，那一行会说明这一点。
- **运行时。** 别的设备发起又停掉的任务按 hub 协议以 `cancelled` 结束，而不是把这条对话上一次的回复当作答案；只有发起的那台设备能停它。动态的第一天用你界面的语言写，而不是英文。回复还在路上时切换模型不再弄断这一轮，新模型从下一轮接手。「连接」里失败的模型测试用平实的话说出错在哪，服务商的原话收在「详情」下面。网页控制台的审批卡用你的语言说明它为什么问，已授予的权限按风险排序，最重的在前。
- **手机上的其他改动。** Android：登录页不再在手机号还没输完的时候就说它「不是大陆号码」；操作屏幕的手会发现自己在绕圈（同一个动作在没变化的屏幕上第三次出现会记进历史，第六次就结束这一轮）；欢迎页说清了电脑今天是怎么加入的。iPhone：「版本」一行在中国大陆也能找到最新版本；第一次运行的登录页在登录成功后自己关上；别的设备交给 iPhone 的任务用那台设备的语言回答；中文文案和 Android 用同样的词（智能体、中继、聊天）；nanoMuse Cloud 放在流里的错误会以它的卡片结束这一轮，而不是「空回复」。两个平台：设置页的 nanoMuse Cloud 一行显示额度还剩多少元，和账号页最上面那个数字一致。
- **中继，0.24.0。** App 一直没有轮询到结束的视频任务（App 被关了、手机睡了）照样计费，只算一次，按提交时定下的价格；没有上游 key 的中继不再为它拒绝的请求在并发计数里占位，以前四次之后账号就被锁在外面；生产环境的自检会报告备份缺失或过期。
- **TestFlight 测试版通过了 Apple 的审核。** 公开链接今天装到的是第 16 个构建（1.0.0）；1.0.1 以第 18 个构建上传，审核通过后链接就会给到它。README、九份翻译和文档都这么写了。

### 升级说明

- **Android 覆盖安装 1.0.0，数据保留。** `nanoMuse-1.0.1-arm64.apk`（Android 8.0+，arm64；versionCode 44）和之前每个版本用同一把签名。校验：`sha256sum -c nanoMuse-1.0.1-arm64.apk.sha256`。
- **App 的 URL scheme 变了。** App 之外的快捷方式、自动化或书签如果打开的是 `minis://…`，现在要改成 `nanomuse://…`（路径不变）；系统不再把 `minis://` 交给 nanoMuse。App 内部的链接和模型写出来的链接不受影响。
- **iPhone 和 iPad 通过 TestFlight。** 公开链接装到的是通过了 Apple 测试版审核的第 16 个构建（1.0.0）；第 18 个构建（1.0.1）上传后送审，通过后链接就会给到它，在那之前先到内部测试员手里。iPhone 上分享扩展和 MCP 登录回调用的是新 scheme；你这边什么都不用做。
- **桌面安装包由 CI 从 tag `v1.0.1` 构建。** Mac 的包没有经过公证：第一次在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」里点「仍要打开」（或右键 → 「打开」）。Windows 的包没有签名：点一次「更多信息」和「仍要运行」。Linux 上优先用 `.deb`；AppImage 需要 FUSE（没有就加 `--appimage-extract-and-run`）。它们比 APK 晚一点到，`SHA256SUMS-desktop.txt` 列出了所有校验值。Windows 的包比 1.0.0 的大一些，多出来的是它现在带着的四段形象短片。
- **中继是 0.24.0，线上什么都没变。** 1.0.0 的客户端对着它照常工作。运维者要知道的新东西：事件表里 `upstream.error` 旁边多了 `content.rejected`，健康页和自检把两者分开报告；自检（`cloud/deploy/nanomuse-hk/selfcheck.sh`）在备份目录里没有 `cloud-*.db.gz`、或者最新的一份超过 `BACKUP_MAX_H` 小时（默认 48）时也会告警，备份在别处就设 `BACKUP_DIR`。没轮询完的视频任务由中继自己结算，只算一次，在这一小时的额度占位到期之前。
