# nanoMuse 0.1.18 · Open

Sign-up is open to everyone, and the app says what it is before it asks for anything.

**nanoMuse is a fully open-source, non-profit community project, built to make a better personal agent together, and it will never charge for anything.** This version is open for the community to try: sign in with an e-mail address and the agent has free model access, about ¥25 (≈ $3.5) a day per account, across chat, pictures and video, paid for by the developer. Your messages are not stored and nothing about you is sold or shared; delete the account at any time, or bring your own API key and skip the relay altogether.

This version also carries everything built since 0.1.16 that had not shipped: nanoMuse Cloud itself (drafted as 0.1.17 "Doorstep", never released), the hub that lets the devices of one account meet, nanoMuse Desktop, video through the relay, and the iOS tree brought back to compiling.

## Added

- **Sign in with e-mail: free.** Anyone can sign up: an e-mail address (or a phone number), a code, and the agent has a chat model, a picture model and a video model on nanoMuse Cloud. Community members have no cap; everyone else has about ¥25 a day, starting over at Beijing midnight. *Settings → nanoMuse Cloud* shows today's spend in ¥ and $, the cap, the total paid by the developer so far, and when the day resets.
- **The notice.** Free, open source, non-profit, what signing in gives, what is and is not kept, on the welcome page, the sign-in page, the account page, the README and [nanomuse.cn](https://nanomuse.cn/).
- **A new first run.** Four pages: the face and one line on what nanoMuse is, with the notice and two doors (*Sign in with e-mail: free* and *I have my own API key*); the models page on the own-key path; the two permissions that let the agent use your phone's apps (accessibility, drawing over other apps), each showing where it stands and opening the right settings page (skippable, everything else works without them); and the hand-off into the first conversation.
- **Approvals in three tiers.** Installing software runs and tells you. Deleting and sending ask first and may be remembered, for this chat, or always for one folder, recipient, site or app. Paying is the highest tier: asked at the moment of paying, every time, unless you choose **Remember and run next time in X**, for one app or site only, confirmed with your screen lock; every payment that then runs on it is said out loud in the chat. *Settings → Permissions* lists everything you remembered, grouped by tier, payments first, tap to revoke. Buying, ordering, booking and trading are fine when you asked for exactly that; nothing unlawful or harmful is done however it is worded: the agent refuses and says why.
- **Costs in the admin console.** What each request cost in ¥ and $ beside its tokens, per account and per day, the price list and the exchange rate; a member toggle per account.
- **nanoMuse Cloud, the hub, Desktop.** The relay (`cloud/`) in the repository, so anyone can run one; devices signed in to one account see each other and can give each other work; nanoMuse Desktop is the computer's Muse with hands on the machine, with installers for Windows, macOS and Linux. The iOS tree compiles on a Mac runner; the iPhone app itself is not released yet. [docs/cloud.md](../cloud.md) · [docs/hub.md](../hub.md) · [docs/desktop.md](../desktop.md) · [docs/ios.md](../ios.md).

## Changed

- **Hands no longer stops at 25 steps.** A task runs until it is done, cannot be done, or you stop it (30 minutes at most).
- **Models that never said whether they see pictures are no longer treated as text-only.** qwen3.8-27b, given a picture, described it instead of saying it could not see it.
- The *nanoMuse Cloud* model group no longer ends up with 0 members on some phones.
- The sign-in page puts e-mail first (a phone number still works).
- versionCode 19; installs over 0.1.16 without losing data.

## Install

Download `nanoMuse-0.1.18-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0 or newer, arm64) and install it over the previous version: same signing key, your data is kept. Verify: `sha256sum -c nanoMuse-0.1.18-arm64.apk.sha256`.
